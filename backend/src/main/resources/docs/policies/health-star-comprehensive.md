# InsurAI Star Comprehensive Health Protection Policy

## 1. Plan Overview
The Star Comprehensive Health Protection Policy provides 360-degree healthcare coverage for individual policyholders and families. It is designed to safeguard families against spiraling medical treatment costs, emergency surgeries, and chronic disease treatments.

## 2. In-Patient Hospitalization Coverage
- **Room Rent & Boarding**: Covered up to Single Private A/C Room category without proportionate deductions.
- **Intensive Care Unit (ICU)**: No sub-limit on ICU charges.
- **Pre-Hospitalization Expenses**: Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days before the hospital admission date are covered.
- **Post-Hospitalization Expenses**: Medical expenses incurred up to 90 days after discharge (including follow-ups, diagnostic scans, and medications) are covered.
- **Day Care Procedures**: Over 405 advanced daycare surgical procedures (such as cataract, chemotherapy, dialysis, lithotripsy, and radiotherapy) requiring less than 24 hours hospitalization are fully covered.

## 3. Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) & Waiting Periods
- **Initial Waiting Period**: 30 days from the initial policy commencement date (except for accidental emergency hospitalization, which is covered from Day 1).
- **Specific Diseases Waiting Period**: A 24-month waiting period applies for treatments of cataracts, hernia, hydrocele, joint replacement, and sinus disorders.
- **Pre-Existing Diseases Waiting Period**: A continuous coverage waiting period of 36 months is mandatory before claims related to declared pre-existing conditions (e.g., Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Asthma) are payable.

## 4. Maternity & Newborn Benefit
- **Maternity Normal Delivery**: Covered up to $2,500 after a continuous policy waiting period of 24 months.
- **Maternity Caesarean Section (C-Section)**: Covered up to $4,000 after 24 months.
- **Newborn Baby Cover**: Automatically covered from Day 1 of birth up to the end of the policy year within the overall maternity limit, including initial mandatory vaccinations.

## 5. Cashless Hospitalization Claims Procedure
1. Locate an empaneled InsurAI Network Hospital via the InsurAI portal or mobile application.
2. Present your InsurAI Digital Health Card and government photo ID at the hospital's TPA / Insurance Helpdesk.
3. The hospital submits a Cashless Pre-Authorization Request electronically to InsurAI.
4. InsurAI Claims Team guarantees review and initial approval within 2 hours.
5. Upon patient discharge, InsurAI settles medical bills directly with the hospital. The policyholder pays only non-medical consumable expenses.

## 6. General Exclusions
- Cosmetic, aesthetic, or obesity treatments unless medically necessitated by post-accident reconstruction.
- Treatments resulting from self-inflicted injuries, suicide attempts, or participation in hazardous extreme sports.
- Experimental, unproven, or non-allopathic alternative treatments unless pre-approved by the Chief Medical Officer.
