# InsurAI Business & SME Commercial Shield Policy

## 1. Plan Overview
InsurAI SME Commercial Shield is tailored for small and medium enterprises, startups, retail outlets, and warehouses. It bundles property, inventory, business interruption, and liability protection under a single comprehensive policy.

## 2. Property & Assets Protection
- **Fire and Special Perils**: Covers commercial building structures, inventory, raw materials, finished stock, office machinery, and fixtures against fire, explosion, aircraft damage, storm, cyclone, flood, and earthquake.
- **Burglary & Housebreaking**: Covers theft of stock and equipment accompanied by forcible or violent entry into premises.
- **Money in Transit & Safe**: Covers cash in safe overnight up to $25,000 and cash in transit to/from banks up to $15,000.

## 3. Business Interruption / Loss of Profit
- **Indemnity Period**: Up to 12 months indemnity protection following covered physical property damage.
- **Coverage Scope**: Reimburses ongoing fixed operating expenses (employee salaries, mortgage/rent payments, taxes, utility standing charges) and net profit lost during the reconstruction/downtime period.

## 4. Commercial Liability Covers
- **Commercial General Liability (Public Liability)**: Covers legal liabilities arising from third-party bodily injury or third-party property damage sustained inside the insured business premises up to $1,000,000 per policy period.
- **Workmen's Compensation / Employer Liability**: Statutory compensation for workplace injuries, permanent disability, or fatalities incurred by enrolled employees during operational duties.

## 5. Cyber Extortion & Data Breach Add-on
- Direct financial indemnification for regulatory breach fines defense, mandatory customer notification costs, data restoration expenses, and ransomware negotiation defense up to $100,000 aggregate limit.

## 6. Claims Filing Protocol
1. Take immediate emergency containment measures to mitigate ongoing damage without compromising safety.
2. Report the incident to InsurAI Corporate Claims desk within 24 hours at business-claims@insurai.com.
3. Preserve the loss site for inspection by the certified loss assessor and insurance surveyor.
4. Submit the claim inventory checklist, audited balance sheets, original purchase bills, and municipal fire brigade / police reports.
