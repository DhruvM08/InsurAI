# InsurAI Term Life Shield Plus Policy

## 1. Plan Overview
InsurAI Term Life Shield Plus is a pure-risk term life assurance policy designed to guarantee financial security and debt clearance for designated family nominees in the unfortunate event of the insured's demise.

## 2. Death Benefit Structure
- **Lump-Sum Payout**: 100% of the insured sum (ranging from $250,000 to $2,000,000) is paid out tax-free to the primary beneficiary upon approval of the death claim.
- **Income Multiplier Option**: If opted at enrollment, 50% is paid as an immediate lump sum, and the remaining 50% is disbursed in monthly indexed installments over 10 years to protect families against inflation.

## 3. Terminal Illness Acceleration Rider
- If the policyholder is diagnosed with an incurable terminal medical condition where certified life expectancy is less than 6 months by two independent medical oncologists/specialists, an accelerated advance payment of 50% of the sum assured (up to $500,000) is disbursed immediately to assist with palliative care.
- The remaining death benefit balance remains active and payable to nominees upon eventual passing.

## 4. Premium Payment Terms & Grace Period
- **Payment Modes**: Annual, semi-annual, quarterly, and monthly auto-debit.
- **Grace Period**: A 30-day grace period is provided for annual, semi-annual, and quarterly payment frequencies (15 days for monthly mode). During the grace period, full life coverage remains active.
- **Policy Lapse & Revival**: If premiums are not paid by the grace period expiration, the policy lapses. Revival is permitted within 2 years upon payment of overdue premiums and submission of a Declaration of Good Health.

## 5. Exclusions & Suicide Clause
- **Suicide Clause**: If the insured passes away by suicide within 12 months from the date of policy commencement or revival, no death benefit is payable. InsurAI will refund 80% of total premiums received (excluding taxes).
- **Subsequent Suicide Coverage**: If demise by suicide occurs after 12 continuous months of an active policy, the full death benefit is recognized and paid to the nominee.
- Demise arising directly from engagement in criminal acts, aviation activities other than as a fare-paying commercial passenger, or active military combat in declared war zones is excluded.

## 6. Death Claim Filing Checklist
Beneficiaries must furnish the following documents to claims@insurai.com:
1. Completed and signed InsurAI Death Claim Form A.
2. Original Death Certificate issued by the Municipal Corporation or Registrar.
3. Original Policy Certificate document.
4. Attending Doctor's Statement and Hospital Discharge/Death Summary.
5. Post-Mortem Report and Police FIR in case of unnatural or accidental death.
6. Nominee's KYC documents and canceled bank check for direct electronic fund transfer.
