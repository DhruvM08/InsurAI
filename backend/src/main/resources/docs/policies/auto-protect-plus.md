# InsurAI Motor Auto Protect Plus Policy

## 1. Plan Overview
InsurAI Auto Protect Plus provides bumper-to-bumper vehicle insurance coverage combining mandatory Third-Party Liability with Comprehensive Own Damage (OD) protection and premium riders.

## 2. Core Coverage
- **Third-Party Bodily Injury & Property Damage**: Unlimited legal liability for third-party bodily injury/death; third-party property damage covered up to $100,000.
- **Own Damage (OD)**: Reimbursement or cashless repair for accidental collision, fire, self-ignition, lightning, theft, and natural disasters (earthquake, flood, storm).
- **Personal Accident Cover**: $50,000 mandatory personal accident cover for the registered vehicle owner-driver.

## 3. Premium Add-On Riders
- **Zero Depreciation (Bumper-to-Bumper)**: Waives standard depreciation on replaced automotive parts during accident repairs. InsurAI settles 100% replacement cost of rubber, plastic, fiberglass, and nylon parts.
- **Engine & Gearbox Protection (Hydrostatic Lock)**: Reimburses costs of engine disassembly, repair, and parts replacement resulting from water ingression or lubricating oil leakage. (Critical for flood and heavy rain incidents where standard policies reject engine water damage).
- **Consumables Cover**: Covers nuts, bolts, engine oil, coolant, washer fluids, and brake fluid used during repair.
- **Roadside Assistance (24x7 Assistance)**: Emergency towing up to 50 km to nearest authorized garage, flat tire replacement, battery jumpstart, emergency fuel delivery up to 5 liters, and vehicle lockout assistance.

## 4. Claims Procedure
1. **Accident / Incident Notification**: Notify InsurAI via mobile app or helpline within 24 hours of occurrence.
2. **Survey & Inspection**: For claims above $500, a digital surveyor assesses damage within 4 hours; instant photo approval for minor scratch and dent claims below $500.
3. **Cashless Network Repair**: Tow vehicle to an authorized InsurAI network garage for cashless settlement.
4. **Deductible**: Compulsory deductible of $50 applies on each own-damage claim.

## 5. Major Exclusions
- Driving without a valid, active driver's license.
- Operating the vehicle under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or toxic substances.
- Using a private registered vehicle for illegal hire, commercial racing, speed testing, or illicit transport.
- Mechanical breakdown, gradual wear and tear, and consequential depreciation.
